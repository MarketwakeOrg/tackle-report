Strategic Insights

What the Data Tells Us

Key Takeaways

  • Tackle is cited in 96.7% of tracked prompts (58/60), meaning nearly every cloud marketplace question AI tools answer includes a link back to tackle.io.
  • Google's AI products (AI Mode, AI Overviews, Gemini) are Tackle's strongest channels, with AI Mode alone achieving an 88.3% brand citation rate.
  • The /cloud-marketplaces and /tackle-platform pages are the two highest-cited URLs, combining for a 176.56 citation score across all platforms.
  • Claude and Meta AI mention Tackle by name (85% and 32.6% presence) but don't provide source citations, representing untapped link-back potential.
  • Blog content accounts for 18.1% of all brand citations, with seller guides and marketplace playbooks driving the highest engagement across AI search.
  • Tackle's brand presence is growing week-over-week, with latest-week presence at 52.1% across all platforms, up from earlier weeks in the tracking period.
TLDR
Tackle dominates AI search for cloud marketplace queries. Google's AI products are the strongest channels. Product pages and blog content are both being actively cited. Claude and Meta mention Tackle by name but don't link to sources, which is a platform limitation, not a Tackle problem.
Explain Like I'm 5
When people ask AI robots about selling software through cloud marketplaces, almost all of them say "Tackle can help with that" and send people to tackle.io. Google's robots do this the most. Some robots (Claude, Meta) talk about Tackle but forget to include the link. Overall, Tackle is doing really well at being the answer AI gives people.
